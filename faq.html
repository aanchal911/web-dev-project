Frequently Asked Questions

What are your plushies made of?

Our plushies are made from high-quality, super-soft polyester fabrics and filled with hypoallergenic polyfill stuffing. We prioritize safety and ensure all materials meet relevant safety standards.

How do I care for my plushie?

We recommend spot cleaning with mild soap and water for minor messes. For a deeper clean, check the care tag on your specific plushie. Some may be machine washable on a gentle cycle in a pillowcase, but air drying is always best to maintain shape and softness.
